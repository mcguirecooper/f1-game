23 − position
Points per driver. 1st = 22 pts, 2nd = 21 … 22nd = 1 pt.
0 pts
DNF, DNS and disqualification all score zero, even if classified.
Driver A + Driver B
Team score = sum of its two drivers. Maximum 43 per race.
