## Design Specificity Verdict

The interface has a credible F1 visual language—dark surfaces, constructor colors, red accent, scoring terminology—but below the hero it becomes a fairly generic analytics dashboard. The visual identity needs a stronger race-week moment and a more obvious answer to “what should I do before the next lock?”

The deterministic detector found one advisory `overused-font` finding at `src/style.css:6` for Inter. It is not a functional defect and may be intentional. The detector found no findings in `index.html`.

## Overall Impression

Desktop is coherent, readable, and appropriately dense for league analytics. The biggest opportunity is to make the product feel like a fantasy game first and a data report second: elevate the next-race pick, current standings context, and lock countdown into the first viewport.

## What's Working

- Strong consistency in dark palette, cards, borders, constructor colors, and tables.
- Clear section structure and useful domain detail: lock timing, history, usage limits, audit flags, and scoring formula.
- The hero establishes the strongest brand expression, while the desktop layout remains calm and scannable.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] The primary action is buried

**Why it matters:** A race-week player must scroll through multiple analysis sections before finding “My pick.” The most consequential action is visually subordinate.

**Fix:** Add a race-week command-center panel directly below the hero with next race, lock time/countdown, current pick status, usage summary, and a clear “Make your pick” action. Keep the full pick section as the detailed destination.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable layout`

### [P1] Mobile hierarchy and navigation are not truly adapted

**Why it matters:** The mobile render spends the first viewport on a large wrapped navigation and oversized hero, while the leaderboard is clipped and the pick action remains far below. The login row can also overflow because inputs retain a 220px minimum width.

**Fix:** Use a compact mobile nav, reduce hero padding/type, make the login form stack, collapse `.admin-grid` and `.form-row`, reduce chart heights, and provide a mobile leaderboard/card view for the highest-value fields.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable adapt`

### [P1] Live/demo authority is too easy to misunderstand

**Why it matters:** Unauthenticated users see demo analytics while authenticated users receive live Supabase data. The single “Demo analytics” chip does not establish which surfaces are authoritative or when data is loading/unavailable.

**Fix:** Add a global data-status treatment with explicit Demo, Live, Loading, and Unavailable states. Repeat the state near leaderboard and pick controls, not only in the hero.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`

### [P1] Core accessibility states are missing

**Why it matters:** Charts have no text equivalent, the login field has no visible label, focus styles are absent, and dynamic status/error messages are not announced to assistive technology.

**Fix:** Add associated labels, `:focus-visible`, `aria-live` status regions, semantic selected/disabled states, chart descriptions or accessible summary tables, and non-color status indicators.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`

### [P2] Analysis is dense without an answer-first summary

**Why it matters:** Title race, player, constructor, and picks charts all look equally important. Users get lots of data but little guidance about current leader, points gap, next race, or available strategic choices.

**Fix:** Add a compact summary row and demote secondary charts behind tabs/details or a lower “Explore the season” section. Surface actionable insights such as constructor uses and points gap.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill`

## Persona Red Flags

- **Casual league player:** The page does not immediately explain what to do this race week or where to begin.
- **Mobile player before lock:** The wrapped nav, oversized hero, dense table, and long scroll make a time-sensitive pick cumbersome.
- **Keyboard/screen-reader player:** Missing visible focus, input labels, chart equivalents, and live-region announcements create avoidable barriers.

## Minor Observations

- Navigation links need larger touch targets, especially on mobile.
- Team and status meaning leans heavily on color; add text/icons/patterns where possible.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` is not handled for smooth scrolling and chart motion.
- Chart instances and resize listeners should be retained/disposed cleanly during live dashboard refreshes.
- Theme colors are duplicated in TypeScript, inline styles, chart options, and CSS tokens.

## Questions to Consider

- Is the primary product goal weekly picking or season analysis, and should the first viewport reflect that choice?
- Should the unauthenticated page be a clearly labeled demo preview, or should live leaderboard context be visible before login?
- Can the next-race module become the signature interaction that gives the league more anticipation and rivalry?
